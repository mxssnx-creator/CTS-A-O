# Live parent admission isolation

All sizing modes, including native lane orders, admit at most one parent per physical symbol and side. The venue uses a netted side and its separate native TP and SL orders are not verified atomic OCO: a second trigger before sibling cancellation can reduce another owner's position even when both orders have bounded quantities. A new parent therefore waits if the physical side contains any own or foreign quantity, or if any own side order, parent, pending fill, stale book, or unresolved identity may still act. A selected first parent on a vacant side retains its folded Block legs; a competing parent cannot join it. Different symbol/sides can continue, and hedge-mode opposite sides remain separate. This guard reduces live concurrency and is not an OCO or fill guarantee.

On a one-way venue, long and short exposure on one symbol cannot physically coexist. A new opposite parent waits until the existing side and its pending orders are confirmed flat. If both directions first appear while the symbol is flat, neither is opened until the conflict is gone. This guard does not change account mode or automatically turn on lane orders. Blocked lanes remain observable in the control funnel with an explanatory reason.

In hedge mode with `foreignSides: independent`, a foreign-only physical side is still unavailable for a new own parent. Quantity bounds cannot prevent an unlinked sibling order from later consuming the foreign quantity. An outside operator can also add quantity after admission; this cannot be prevented by the desk and requires reconciliation. Existing foreign positions and their orders are not deliberately adopted or canceled.

Legacy sides already holding multiple parents are not retrospectively divided or forcibly closed. Their old native orders and tracked parent identities, including a parent with an incomplete native-order map, remain reserved while exact native fills are reconciled; changes in market price or one paper lane disappearing do not resize the survivor or fabricate a market close. Existing overlapping venue orders can still race with each other before the desk can cancel them, so this safety hold does not retroactively isolate them. Such positions may need operator reconciliation and can delay a paper time or trailing exit. Unavailable or cancelled native protection must be treated as unresolved; a whole-side backstop is not equivalent to each parent's own stop.

Existing mixed aggregate positions are **not** retroactively divided into inferred parent quantities; their ambiguity is surfaced in control status. Their whole owned side and existing backstop are retained, and all old parent identities survive across steps and restarts, until reconciliation or a durably recorded full-close intent establishes what to do. A recorded full-close intent can retry or finish its confirmed remaining owned quantity; a rejection or pending response is never treated as a fill. Exact per-parent owned quantities and fills remain unavailable without a durable, confirmed-fill allocation ledger. A held position without a recoverable parent record is also conservatively retained, so its individual time/target/stop transition may require reconciliation. Venue minimums, conditional-order caps, rejected or delayed orders, gaps, downtime and emergency position-wide backstops prevent a guarantee that a lane executes at its requested price. These restrictions concern native admission and do not prove profitability.

A native exit that fills during cancellation remains pending until the same venue order supplies its actual quantity and fill price; a flat position alone does not make a ticker price into an own close. Multiple verified partial exits for one parent retain cumulative quantity and execution notional across restart, so the settled price is quantity-weighted. A mixed stop/target exit is labeled with the final fill's reason; that label does not describe every slice. Old partials lacking reliable cumulative notional remain unresolved. The reduction journal retains a measured commission when supplied and marks an absent commission unknown; later matching venue history can fill that gap. The separate per-lane return still uses the configured or aggregate measured cost model, not an exact allocation of each entry's and exit's fees.

After a partial native execution, both old oversized conditionals are reconciled by their exact identities; a cancellation acknowledgment alone is insufficient. If both are terminal and a later fresh venue book and the owned ledger agree on the sole parent's exact remaining quantity, the desk can place the saved stop at that quantity, followed by the saved target after the stop is observed. Unresolved cancellation, a rejected or uncertain placement, foreign or other parent quantity, stale snapshots, venue limits, and prices past the saved level leave the parent pending. During the interval, or if the backstop is missing, the exact native stop may be absent: the status is not a claim that it is protected at its configured level. This does not guarantee fills during outages or exchange races.

A refusal saying a position stop already exists is not proof that any particular old order is stale. Recovery never cancels every order on a side; if the just-opened fill cannot obtain its required stop, the bounded emergency close path applies. Only the explicit position-stop order kind is treated as backstop protection; pending entry and market-close orders are not stops and must not be removed by stop resizing.

The desk does not cancel or swap existing native stops or targets merely to make room under the venue-order cap. New protection may therefore remain pending until a slot becomes available. A parent disappearing from Paper is retained with its old native orders even while lane-order mode remains enabled; this avoids canceling protection before a partial or refused market close, but delays Paper-only exits until its native exit or reconciliation. Flat unjournaled orders are not presumed stale. Once an exact native close is established, a position-wide order can be canceled only by its own journaled identity, and a durable reservation prevents new exposure until a terminal zero-fill receipt confirms the cancellation.

Switching off native lane orders does not cancel existing per-lane exits: the desk stays in a drain state, blocks new parents, and retains the original stop and target identities, levels and confirmed quantities across restart. If a native exit is confirmed cancelled with zero execution, the desk can replace it at that saved level and quantity when the whole tracked quantity is still confirmed on the venue. An unknown order, unknown execution, inconsistent fill information, missing level or unverified ownership requires reconciliation instead of a guessed replacement. When a paper parent disappears during this drain, the desk waits for that parent's own venue exit or explicit ownership reconciliation. It does not cancel the native exits to submit a market reduction whose refusal could leave the parent unprotected. This can delay a paper-triggered time or trailing exit until the venue order fires or an operator reconciles it. A native order without a recoverable parent map is retained for reconciliation; its quantity is not guessed. Once all native orders are reconciled and the old lanes have left, aggregate mode resumes.

The Paper reconciliation guard also requires exact valid `+1`/`-1` direction, config, symbol and entry identity before accepting a simulated close. A malformed side is not normalized into another order's close or capacity release.

An aggregate residual whose target was suppressed after an outside partial reduction recovers the last confirmed stop level from its own position journal, after the old cancellation is confirmed. It is bounded to the venue quantity step and never widened to a generic default. An unknown level, missing quantity step, crossed price, incompatible venue/liquidation constraint or refused replacement remains pending instead of triggering an invented level or a market close. During the cancellation/replacement interval, or while a receipt is unresolved, the exact residual stop may be absent; the status is not a guarantee of protection.

Stop-level recovery requires a proven positive opening in the same episode. New openings and stops use their journaled pre-dispatch sequence, which survives cumulative order updates and distinguishes equal timestamps. An unsequenced legacy tie, a flat marker tied to another event, a contradictory clock history mixed with unsequenced records, or a missing opening leaves the level unresolved; it is not inherited from an earlier parent. This can delay protection until the operator reconciles the old records.

When an aggregate own-first outside reduction exhausts a hedge side beside physical foreign quantity, only an older, exact journaled own stop or target may be retired. This is a conservative ledger decision, not proof of the identity or price of the venue fill. A durable cancellation reservation remains until an exact terminal zero-execution receipt; uncertain or partial execution stays unresolved. Foreign orders and unjournaled lookalikes are not canceled. A prior trusted foreign-quantity estimate is carried through a foreign-order freeze rather than treating the whole mixed position as foreign. Old frozen snapshots with no trustworthy estimate remain held across restart for reconciliation, even if the foreign order disappears. The desk cannot prevent a venue-side trigger or operator change between reads.

Account positions use the exchange snapshot after the own ledger is capped and that snapshot's outside reductions are reconciled. A mixed side's known shares and the corresponding unrealized P&L and margin are estimated pro rata; the venue's whole-position P&L does not independently prove each owner's P&L. Frozen or pending shares are shown as unresolved rather than zero, excluded from own and foreign subtotals, and make the session's complete open and net P&L unavailable. The snapshot precedes orders submitted later in the same step, so a filled reduction appears on a subsequent account read. Tiny arithmetic residue within a few floating-point ulps and much less than the venue quantity tolerance is normalized only when a measured outside reduction otherwise exhausts the own quantity; a meaningful remainder is retained.

An unexplained shortfall on a side with an outstanding native lane stop or target is held for exact native-order reconciliation. In a mixed physical position, a partial native fill can remain in the open-orders book, masked by foreign quantity; treating that shortfall as a separate outside reduction could subtract it twice. The earlier foreign baseline is retained while the identity remains unresolved, and no market action is sent for that key. Existing protection may still race or be unavailable; this hold is not atomic execution protection.

After native reconciliation the account display uses a fresh reporting-only ledger. Repeated physical rows for the same side remain visible, with their side-level own split marked unknown; a single side-level quantity is never assigned to every row. An accepted entry or other unresolved key with no position row is counted separately from physical positions and also makes complete session open and net P&L unavailable.

A missing or invalid venue position unrealized-P&L mark stays unknown, distinct from uncertain ownership. Own session open and net P&L remain unavailable until every known own share has a mark; an explicit venue zero is still zero. The separate whole-account balance parser and account-wide `openNet` still default a missing aggregate unrealized-P&L field to zero; those account-wide values are therefore not a verified mark when the balance response omits it.

An older persisted account snapshot does not prove that it had valid ownership and position marks. Its own session and mark subtotals remain unavailable until a fresh read explicitly records zero unknown ownership, pending keys and missing marks. Only numeric decimal or scientific-notation position P&L values are accepted.

After an exactly measured native partial, an older journaled aggregate stop or target that exceeds the remaining own quantity is retired through the exact-identity cancellation reservation. New submission identities are sequenced before dispatch, so equal millisecond timestamps do not erase their causal ordering within the retained journal; old identities lacking sequence use strict timestamp evidence and ties remain unknown. A later entry, ambiguous chronology, stale book, missing identity or unjournaled order cannot authorize cancellation. Its confirmed zero-execution receipt is required; a refusal, nonterminal status or unknown execution stays pending and is not repeatedly submitted. While native reconciliation remains busy, aggregate protection is not automatically repaired or resized. This can leave the residual without its former aggregate stop. If the venue triggers the old oversized order before cancellation, or its identity cannot be reconciled, it can still reduce foreign quantity: neither residual protection nor exclusion of every foreign fill is guaranteed.

The default runtime does not configure `CTS_CORE_SNAPSHOT`: in that mode the SQLite ownership and submission-sequence tables are memory-only and `runDurable` does not make them restart-safe. With a configured snapshot, startup applies the journal transactionally only after restoring the snapshot. A malformed journal, or a missing/corrupt snapshot alongside recovery artifacts, stops both live execution modes and leaves existing venue orders untouched until reconciliation; an unproven journal suffix is not replayed on its own. A state-file checkpoint alone does not retain all live-order rows. The restart tests that establish sequence recovery use a configured state/journal or file-backed test database, and do not prove power-loss durability. Live use requires this persistence gap to be resolved; a missing `lastJournalError` is not proof of durable storage.
