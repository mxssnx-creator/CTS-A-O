Four additional Signal catalog candidates were defined in the original [research protocol](signal-research-protocol-20261015.json).
The [corrected v2 protocol](signal-research-protocol-20261016.json) preserves those formulas and fixes runtime lane-trail
eligibility; the original performance report is withdrawn pending a rerun. All candidates start disabled. The original
96 catalog sources and 63 enabled defaults remain intact. A formula citation establishes provenance, not profitability.

| Source                      | Frozen short / medium settings                          | Calculation and provenance                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Laguerre RSI turn           | gamma 0.5, levels 0.15/0.85 / gamma 0.7, levels 0.1/0.9 | Reuses `research3.ts` unchanged. [John Ehlers' author paper](https://www.88broker.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/01/timewarp.pdf), pp. 3–5, supplies the four-stage Laguerre recurrence. | ATR Chandelier channel flip | 22 bars × 3 ATR / 44 bars × 3.5 ATR                     | Reuses `research3.ts` unchanged, using the previous completed bar's rolling extremes and ATR. | Klinger volume-force cross  | 34/55/13 / 55/89/21                                     | Reuses the existing cumulative-volume-force variant unchanged: volume×abs(2×dm/cm−1)×trend×100, then fast-minus-slow EMA and a signal EMA; crosses point away from zero. [Trading Technologies](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/trade/analytics/charts/technical-indicators/klinger-volume-oscillator-kvo/) documents a simpler signed-volume version. These are not claimed to be identical formulas. |
| Relative Vigor Index cross  | 10 / 20 periods                                         | New `research4.ts` follows [MetaQuotes' official RVI calculation](https://www.metatrader5.com/en/terminal/help/indicators/oscillators/rvi): apply symmetric 1,2,2,1 weights to close-minus-open and high-minus-low, divide their period sums, then a normalized four-bar signal line with the same weights. Positive-RVI upward crosses enter long; negative-RVI downward crosses enter short. The sign alignment is the frozen system entry rule.                                                                                                                                                            |

RVI uses completed OHLC bars only. A nonfinite/invalid bar or time gap restarts warmup; a zero denominator stays
unknown and cannot create an entry. Short and medium wrappers retain separate full source identities, lanes,
protections and acceptance samples. Existing research formulas are preserved rather than silently rewritten.

Registration also makes the new RVI base indications available to engine configuration discovery. The separate
six-hour baseline retains its frozen original engine focus and 63 enabled Signals; it does not add these candidates
to its coordinator universe. The new-source study uses its own frozen chronological protocol, keeps losing selected
members and endpoint marks, and must disclose modeled costs and return-PF sample counts. No source is promoted
automatically from this registration or from a winning reported subset.
