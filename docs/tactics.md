# Tactics and presets

All numbers are on real BingX perpetual data with the 0.2 % round-trip cost on every close. Every run is
causal: signals come from closed bars, entries fill at the next bar's open, and SL is checked before TP inside a bar.

## 1. Adjusting every indication one by one (`scripts/core-adjust.mjs`, `docs/adjust-1h.md`)

All 98 indications × {follow, revert} × 17 causal filters × 2 protects on a year of 1h bars from 40 symbols.
Half A was used for selection and half B for validation.

- Of 6664 variants, **22 pass PF ≥ 1.1 in both halves**. Nearly all are the RSI-extreme family.
- No filter rescues a losing indication on its own. Across all indications the best filters (volatility regime,
  EMA50 slope, ADX 25) improve both halves in only 34–38 % of cases, and the pooled PF moves from 0.84 to about 0.86.

## 2. The family that works: RSI-extreme momentum (`scripts/core-family.mjs`, `docs/family-1h.md`)

The signal enters **with** the move on the bar where RSI crosses into an extreme (above 100−L long, below L short).
The scan covered RSI period × level × TP × SL ratio × hold × trail × filter: 9216 variants on 1h.

- **2206 of 8640 pass PF ≥ 1.1 in both halves.** RSI 21 has a median half-B PF of 1.28.
- The best settings are wide targets (7–10 % TP), SL 1.5–2.5 × TP and a 24–48 h hold.
- On 15m the family is much weaker (38 of 8640 pass). 1h is its timeframe.

It was added to the engine as seven indications, `rsi-mom-{10-25,14-15,14-20,14-25,21-15,21-20,21-25}`, traded with the `follow` bot.

## 3. Tactics (Settings → Tactics; every one can be switched off)

| tactic | rule |
|---|---|
| session | EU/US session: only signals on bars opening 07:00–20:59 UTC |
| volRegime | ATR% in the upper half of its last 336 bars (2 weeks on 1h) |
| trendStrength | ADX(14) ≥ 20 |
| cooldown | after an exit the config waits N bars before it re-enters the symbol |

Tactics filter the signal of every combo in Base, Main, Real and Live alike. They only ever remove entries.
With all of them off the engine computes the plain signals. The engine adds the warm-up the tactics need
(for example the 2-week volatility rank) to the history it evaluates.

Measured in the complete engine: fixed momentum set, 175 separate 48 h walk-forward runs per year, 1h bars, 40 symbols.
The prior year (Sep 2024 → Sep 2025) was never used by any selection.

| tactic · execution | research year PF | orders/day | prior year PF | orders/day |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| none · Normal | 1.15 | 12 | 1.01 | 11 |
| volRegime · Normal | 1.07 | 8 | **1.11** | 8 |
| volRegime · Normal + Trailing | 1.06 | 9 | 1.04 | 8 |
| session + volRegime · Normal | **1.16** | 7 | 1.05 | 6 |
| session + volRegime · Normal + Trailing | 1.12 | 7 | 1.00 | 6 |
| session · Normal | 1.07 | 10 | 0.98 | 9 |
| trendStrength · Normal | 1.10 | 11 | 1.01 | 11 |
| cooldown · Normal | 1.11 | 11 | 1.03 | 11 |

The full tables, including Trailing only and Block Active + DCA Active, are in `docs/tactics/tac-{cur,prev}-*.md`.

What the data shows:

- **The volatility regime is the one tactic that holds in both years.** Session helps only in the research year.
- **Trailing** raises the share of green hours by 2–3 points but lowers PF slightly. Trailing on its own loses in the prior year.
- **Block Active + DCA Active** does not beat plain Normal on this family.
- Earlier runs (15m, all combos, durable selection) scored PF 0.82–0.94. The momentum family on 1h is the first
  setup that stays at or above break-even out of time. Its edge is small: PF 1.0–1.16, not a guaranteed profit.

## 4. Selection mode "fixed"

A research-validated set trades continuously. The durable gate is off, so it does not wait for a 14-day proof
that cuts orders by about 60 %. Per pair, the engine picks the protect × sub-strategy with the best lower
confidence bound on the long window. Last-N, Block and the caps still apply.

## 5. Presets (`/v2/presets`)

- **Research presets** are generated by `scripts/core-presets-gen.mjs` from the measured runs above. Each shows PF,
  the success ratio (green hours), green days, positive runs, trades per day, win rate, net, the longest drawdown
  and the out-of-time result on the prior year.
- **Save preset** stores the current settings together with the latest simulated run's results.
- **Auto presets:** after each compute whose simulated run is stable, meets the minimum PF and has enough trades,
  the settings are saved automatically. There is one preset per distinct setting and the best run is kept.
- **Apply** replaces the timeframe, focus, grid, tactics, strategies and selection. It never touches the Live stage.
