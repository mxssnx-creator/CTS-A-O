# Low-Range Short-only retrospective follow-up

This reruns the 24-hour research protocol in `PROTOCOL-24H.md` with only
the Short range tag. The feed, time boundaries, indicators, independent lanes,
cost model, cell gates and direction evaluation are otherwise unchanged.
The earlier three-range study has already been observed, so this is a
retrospective scope reduction, not a fresh holdout or independent trial.

The output is an overlapping, unallocated candidate-tape diagnostic. It does
not simulate portfolio sizing, venue fills, or a production runtime session.
Every hour and every symbol must be retained, including empty and losing
observations. Reports must distinguish the historical post-Base validation
and PF from subsequent candidate outcomes and must not promise profitability.
