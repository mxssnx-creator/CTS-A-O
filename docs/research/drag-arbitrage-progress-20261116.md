# Drag, Arbitrage and continuous stage processing — 16 November 2026

## Scope and ownership

Drag has eight independent BTC-relative signal profiles: a 20- or 40-bar causal correlation/beta fit, two lag ratios, and catch-up or confirmation. The 1m, 5m and 15m lanes consume aligned completed BTC-USDT and symbol bars; a missing, ambiguous, stale/gapped reference produces no signal until its own window has warmed again. New factory settings select BTC as a normal universe symbol. The Settings page can add both Drag focus and BTC to an existing selection. Each Drag profile uses the ordinary configured protection ranges and the existing exact config/symbol/side stage gates. A separately configured or named Drag strategy-range grid has not been implemented.

Arbitrage is currently a **quote-only research calculator**, not a directional indication, a strategy coordination, or an exchange order adapter. It finds three-currency cycles independently per venue and estimates both directions at bid/ask with top-book quantities, quantity steps, minimum order rules, one taker fee per leg and adverse slippage. Age, clock skew and edge limits are saved as research settings. It cannot provide live order placement, partial-fill recovery, hedge handling, or fill reconciliation. Quotes must be supplied in the required format; the OHLCV feed does not establish synchronized executable triangular opportunities. Its defaults are cautious research inputs, not calibrated optimal trading parameters.

## Stage fixes and their meaning

* Base-qualified Signal configs can gather their own stage-only samples even while their own acceptance or loss guard is immature or paused. Confirmation may also be deferred for **stage-only** research. Each funded admission continues to require the actual acceptance, guard, confirmation, Real and other entry rules.
* Unfunded stage-only positions have their own duplicate tracking and no longer consume funded capacity, enter funded conflict/crowd counts, or prevent another independent configuration from advancing. The replay and both full/incremental paper paths have regression fixtures for this distinction.
* Stage-only orders are hypothetical and their PF is not execution PF. In particular, a Signal's unconfirmed samples do not establish that confirmed executions will have the same PF. Report separately and do not treat model counts as exchange fills.
* Signal progress counts Base-proven configs before the active-seat filter and does not count missing Base provenance. Incremental continuation is bounded to 8,192 additional config/symbol/direction contexts; the visible `waiting` count represents eligible contexts that are not continuously tracked in that generation.
* Paper candidates are published only after their stage checkpoint has been persisted; stale real-time entries use wall-clock freshness. Large model snapshots are time-sliced, and replay model identifiers reuse shared lane values and omit repeated config text from a parent ID whose identity already contains the config, symbol and side.

## Offline evidence

The four-symbol, 12-hour prehistory plus 12-hour BTC/DOT/APT/ARB Drag-only replay evaluated 24 profiles/lanes with a reduced Minimal/Short grid. None passed Base. With default tactics, the volatility filter requires 336 prior completed lane bars, exceeding the 12-hour sample for 5m and 15m. An explicit tactics-off comparison produced entries on those lanes, but again 0/24 passed Base. Passing PF and funded PF are unavailable; no positive outcome is implied.

A controlled four-symbol comparison of two Signal sources and a reduced grid recorded 0 modeled post-Base closes before the Signal-learning changes and 60 after them. All 192 exact contexts still had insufficient samples for the 12-close first stage and there were no funded trades. The persistent full and incremental paper paths were separately exercised with deterministic long/short fixtures; this does not prove a profitable market run.

An unrestricted four-symbol replay with Normal, Block/Block Active, Axis and all configured Signals was terminated by the environment at 6/12 trading intervals with last logged RSS around 2 GB; it has no final result. A narrower Signal-source replay is required on this environment. The previous full-catalog run completed before these newest changes but cannot establish the performance or profitability of the present revision.

A later bounded replay on the same 12+12-hour window used BTC, DOT, APT and ARB, the configured ordinary focus, two Signal sources (EMA-cross and RSI-reversal), and no Signal Coordination. Normal, Block/Block Active and Axis were enabled; Exit followed the configured switch. It completed 76/76 report integrity checks in 143.3 seconds wall time, with 1,627 MB peak logged RSS and a 1,226 ms maximum observed event-loop delay. It is a smaller Signal catalog than the terminated run and does not prove the requested full workload meets memory or latency targets.

There were 37 Base-passing pairs out of 2,428 evaluated and 11,648 built tapes. At every hourly snapshot 0/11,516 configuration checks passed; the last snapshot recorded 11,504 insufficient-close failures and 12 net failures. The separate modeled persistent-stage book recorded 350,327 post-Base closes; 254/93,184 exact contexts (0.273%) qualified at its first window, while none of those contexts had completed a qualifying Real window. No funded position or order executed, so passing and funded PF are unavailable. All 12 reporting hours were flat. This replay's isolated endpoint paper checkpoint was empty after 20,167 stale entries were rejected; continuous persistent paper and venue execution are not established by this market replay.

A same-feed sensitivity run used Main 15 and range Last-50 (the lowest value accepted by current settings), while keeping the persistent stages 12/12 and the PF floors unchanged. Two of 11,516 configuration checks (0.017%) passed in each of six hourly snapshots; both passing sets had no losses in their reported samples, so no finite passing PF was available. The two-stage model remained at 254/93,184 first-window qualified contexts and zero completed Real windows; there were no funded orders. This single-window comparison does not justify changing the factory defaults.
