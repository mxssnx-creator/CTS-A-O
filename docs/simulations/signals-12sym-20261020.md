## Scope and changes

Signals retain their own 336h broad history independently of engine 24h. Physical retention/backfill includes indicator warmup and the simulation span; extended Signal lane universes do not expand ordinary engine cohorts. Explicit evaluation boundaries remain causal. Existing short caches are extended progressively without repeatedly retrying a usable young history ahead of later batches.

Exact source/range/lane/type/config/symbol/side evidence remains independent. A repeated symbol name in a multi-timeframe universe no longer causes Last-N to select the first index while its trades use the last index. Native admission rechecks current own validation/Last-N, in addition to activation and verified owned evidence. Exit-only held configurations do not authorize fresh entries.

Settings expose `signals.trailMax` from 0.002 to 0.03, default 0.03. The entry-distance ceiling covers Signal initial stop, activation and entry-anchored trailing giveback; each configuration carries its own ceiling in its ID. Legacy IDs preserve 2%. Historical fitting scores the final capped protection. DCA/Axis and native projections preserve the exact cap. Actual realized losses may exceed the distance through gaps and costs; no loss is clamped out of statistics. A separate feed correction removes the fixed 20-page / 480h ceiling for longer preset history requests, while keeping a bounded guard for sparse/broken venue replies. A 600h / 36,000-bar regression passes.

Three opt-in sources are implemented with short/medium variants and all four lanes: APZ reclaim, FRAMA pullback and Supertrend pullback. They remain disabled in production defaults. Formula references and frozen entry adaptations are in [signal-research-20261020.md](../signal-research-20261020.md). Literature is not return evidence.
